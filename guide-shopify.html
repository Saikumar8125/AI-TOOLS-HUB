FAQ

Is Shopify Sidekick free in India?

It's included with Shopify plans; features and limits vary by plan.

Can Sidekick write product descriptions on mobile?

No, description generation is desktop only.

What does GoKwik cost for a small brand?

Emerging plan: ₹3,000/month for up to 2,000 orders, plus 3.5% on COD transactions.

What are AiSensy's WhatsApp message charges?

Marketing ₹1.09 and utility/authentication/service ₹0.145 per message, charged separately.

Is there a free GST accounting option?

Zoho Books has a free plan for businesses under ₹25 lakh revenue, including GST reports and filing.
