No endorsement, sponsorship or affiliate links

Accuracy of tool information

Descriptions, categories, pricing labels and India status are checked in good faith on the dates shown on the site (for example "Links checked" and "India facts checked" dates) and can go out of date. A tool marked "India availability not verified" means we have not confirmed INR pricing, UPI, GST invoices or Indian-language support on its official pages; it does not mean the tool is unavailable. Platforms are shown as "Not verified" unless stated. Always confirm on the official website.

Your data in third-party tools

When you sign up for or upload information to a tool, its maker handles that data under its own terms and privacy policy, which may involve storage outside India. Before you put customer or employee data into any tool, check how it handles personal data and whether that fits your own obligations under Indian law.

AI output

AI tools can produce wrong, biased or made-up results. Review anything an AI tool produces before you publish it, send it to customers or rely on it for legal, tax, medical or financial matters.
