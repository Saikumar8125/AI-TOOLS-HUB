Why the directory names other companies' tools

The site is an informational and editorial directory. To help small businesses find and compare tools, we have to say what each tool is called, show which tool we mean and describe it. Names and logos are used only to identify each tool and link to its official site.

Third-party trademarks

All product names, logos, brands and trademarks shown on this site belong to their respective owners. We claim no ownership of them and no right to use them beyond identifying the tools. Their appearance here gives no one, including us, permission to use them for any other purpose.

No partnership or endorsement

A listing does not mean that a tool's maker endorses, sponsors, partners with or is affiliated with AI Tools Hub, or that we endorse the tool. Labels such as "Featured" or "Trending picks" are our own editorial picks. See also the Third-Party Disclaimer.

Logos, icons, fonts and images

  • Tool logos are each website's own favicon. They are not stored in our files. Your browser loads them when a page is shown, from Google's favicon service (www.google.com/s2/favicons), at small size and as decoration next to the tool's name. If a logo cannot load, the first letter of the tool's name is shown instead. If you own a logo and would rather it not appear, tell us and we will show the letter instead.
  • Interface icons are from Lucide (version 0.468.0), used under the ISC License. They are kept in our own icons.svg file with the licence notice.
  • Fonts: Geist and Geist Mono (SIL Open Font License 1.1), self-hosted. Apple devices show their own built-in system font instead; it is not distributed by this site.
  • Site logo and share image (icon.svg, og-image.png) were made for this site.
  • We do not use product screenshots or other images copied from tool websites.

Our own content

Tool descriptions, categories, guides and the selection of tools are written and arranged by us, and we aim to write them in our own words. The site's source code is published on GitHub under the MIT License shown there. Quoting short parts of the site with a link back is welcome.

How to submit a complaint

If you believe something on this site infringes your copyright or trademark, or misrepresents your product, send a complaint to aitoolshub.support@gmail.com. Please include:

  1. your name and how to contact you, and, if you act for someone else, who you represent;
  2. the right concerned (for example the trademark, its registration number if any, or the copyrighted work);
  3. the exact page address or addresses and what on each page is the problem;
  4. what you would like us to do (correct, add attribution, replace the logo, or remove);
  5. a statement that the information in your complaint is accurate and that you are the rights holder or authorised to act for them.

How complaints are reviewed

We read and reply to complaints every Sunday, between 9 am and 5 pm (India time, IST). We check the pages named, compare them with the official source or the rights you describe, and may ask for more information or proof of rights. Where a complaint affects someone else (for example a reviewer of a tool), we may share its substance with them so they can respond. We decide in good faith and tell you the outcome.

How disputed content is corrected or removed

  • Wrong facts (name, link, pricing label, description) are corrected when an official source confirms the right information.
  • Logos can be replaced with the letter fallback on request.
  • Disputed content may be hidden while we review it, where that is reasonable.
  • Listings can be removed if the complaint is upheld or the maker asks for removal.

Changes are made in the site's public source and recorded in the project history on GitHub. If you disagree with a decision, reply with your reasons and any evidence and we will look at it again.
