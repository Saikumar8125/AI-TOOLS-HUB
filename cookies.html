Cookie Policy

Last updated 2 Nov 2026

This page is a plain-language draft and has not yet been reviewed by a lawyer.

Short answer: this site does not set any cookies. The site's code never writes cookies, and in a check of the live site on 2 Nov 2026 its responses set none. Because the site sets no cookies and runs no tracking, it does not show a cookie banner.

What the site uses instead: localStorage

To remember your theme, saved tools, compare picks and recently viewed tools, the site uses your browser's localStorage. Unlike cookies, localStorage is not sent to any server with your requests. It stays on your device. The keys are listed in the Privacy Policy. The optional interface sounds are small files loaded from this site itself, only after you turn sound on.

Third-party requests

Your browser contacts a few other services to show the site. We do not ask them to set cookies, but we do not control them:

  • Google favicon service (tool logos from www.google.com). Depending on your browser's settings, your browser may attach cookies it already holds for google.com to these requests. We cannot see or read those cookies.
  • GitHub, only when you follow a GitHub link. GitHub uses its own cookies on its site.
  • Tool websites, only when you press Visit. Each site sets its own cookies under its own policy.

How to control this

Use the button above, or clear "site data" for aitoolshub-in.vercel.app in your browser settings. Most browsers can also block third-party cookies or requests; the site still works if tool logos are blocked (a letter appears instead).

Changes

If the site ever starts setting cookies, this page will be updated first, with what each cookie does and how long it lasts.
