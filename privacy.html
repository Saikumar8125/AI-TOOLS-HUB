Short version

  • No accounts, no sign-up, no database and no forms that send anything to this site.
  • No analytics, advertising or session-recording scripts are present in the site's code.
  • Your theme, saved tools, compare picks and recently viewed tools stay in your own browser (localStorage).
  • Your browser does contact a few other services to show the site: Vercel (our host) and Google's favicon service. They receive basic technical data such as your IP address.
  • Suggestions and problem reports open GitHub, where you choose whether to post. Posts there are public.

What this site collects

The site is a set of static files. It has no login, no user accounts, no database and no server code that receives form data. We do not ask for your name, email address or phone number anywhere on the site.

Like every website, the pages are delivered by a hosting provider that sees technical request data. That is explained under Hosting and third parties.

Search, filters and the page address

Search, the Tool Finder and the directory filters run in your browser. The site search downloads one file, /search-index.json, which is the same for every visitor; what you type is matched on your device and is not sent with that request.

To make results shareable, the directory writes your search words and filters into the page address (for example /?q=invoice&price=free). If you reload, bookmark or share that address, the full address, including the search words, is requested from our host like any other page address.

Analytics and tracking

As of 2 Nov 2026, the site's code contains no Google Analytics, no Vercel Web Analytics or Speed Insights script, no advertising or social media pixels, and no session-recording tools. We do not build visitor profiles.

If we ever add analytics, we will update this page before it goes live and describe what it collects.

Data kept on your device (localStorage)

A few small settings are kept in your browser's localStorage so the site remembers them between visits. They are read only by the site's own scripts in your browser and are never sent to us or to anyone else. They contain tool names and a theme word, nothing about you.

Recently viewed tools and favourites

Recently viewed (up to 8 tools) is recorded when you open a tool's details on the home page or visit a tool page. Favourites are recorded only when you press Save. Both lists live in localStorage as described above, so they exist only on the device and browser where you made them. We cannot see them and cannot restore them if they are deleted.

Suggestion and report forms

The "Suggest a tool" form on the home page checks your entries in your browser and then opens a new GitHub issue page in a new tab, with your text filled in. Nothing is sent to this site. The "Report a problem" and "Report a wrong link or price" links work the same way. The "Copy details" button only copies the text to your clipboard.

Posting on GitHub needs a GitHub account and is your choice. An issue you post is public and shows your GitHub username. GitHub's privacy statement applies there. Please do not include personal details, phone numbers or customer data in an issue.

Hosting and third parties

Your browser contacts these services when you use the site. Each receives standard technical data that any web request carries: your IP address, the address requested, the time, and your browser's user-agent. Each handles that data under its own policy, which we do not control.

  • Vercel hosts and delivers the site, including the short interface sound files (click.mp3, open.mp3, add.mp3, success.mp3), which load from this site itself and only after you turn sound on. It may record request data in its logs, under its own retention rules. Vercel privacy policy.
  • Fonts: Apple devices use their built-in system font (San Francisco). Other devices use Geist and Geist Mono, served from this site itself, so no font request goes to a third party.
  • Google favicon service (www.google.com/s2/favicons) supplies the small tool logos. Each request names the tool's website domain, so Google can see which tool logos your browser loaded. Our pages ask the browser to send no referrer with these requests. Google privacy policy.
  • GitHub is contacted only when you follow a GitHub link, such as Suggest a tool, Report a problem or Source on GitHub.
  • Tool websites: when you press Visit, you leave this site. Your browser may tell that site you came from aitoolshub-in.vercel.app (the site name only, under our strict-origin-when-cross-origin referrer setting). That site's own privacy policy then applies.

Cookies

This site does not set cookies. Details, including what third parties may do, are in the Cookie Policy.

Retention and deletion

  • Your browser: localStorage stays until you delete it (button above or browser settings).
  • This site: we keep no database, so there is no copy of your settings on our side to delete.
  • Hosting logs: kept by Vercel for the period its service sets. This site has no database or server code of its own that stores them.
  • GitHub issues: stay public until you or the repository owner delete them. You can edit or delete your own comments, and you can ask us to delete an issue you posted in our repository.

Security

The site is served only over HTTPS, and the host sends a Strict-Transport-Security header. The site handles no passwords, payments or personal records. It loads no third-party scripts: the only files from other services are the tool logos, and the logos are requested without a referrer. No website can promise perfect security, but keeping no personal data is the main safeguard here.

Your choices and rights

You can use the whole site without giving any personal information. You can clear stored settings at any time, block third-party requests in your browser, and decide whether to post anything on GitHub.

India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 gives people rights to information about, correction of and erasure of their personal data, to grievance redressal and to nominate someone to act for them. These rights take effect on the phased dates described below. Because this site keeps no personal data about visitors, there is usually nothing on our side to access or erase, but you can still write to [OWNER EMAIL — to be added] and we will tell you what, if anything, applies.

India's data protection law: where things stand

The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 was enacted on 11 August 2023. The government published the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025 in the Gazette of India on 13 November 2025 (G.S.R. 846(E)) and announced them on 14 November 2025. A separate notification the same day (G.S.R. 843(E)) brings the Act into force in three phases:

  • From 13 November 2025: definitions and the provisions setting up the Data Protection Board of India (Act sections 18 to 26 and others; Rules 1, 2 and 17 to 21).
  • One year later, around 13 November 2026: Consent Manager provisions (section 6(9), section 27(1)(d) and Rule 4).
  • Eighteen months later, around 13 May 2027: most duties of organisations and rights of individuals, including notice, consent, security safeguards, breach reporting, children's data and grievance handling (sections 3 to 17, 27 to 34 and others; Rules 3, 5 to 16, 22 and 23).

The later dates are counted from the publication date and could change if the government amends the notification. Sources: PIB press release, 14 Nov 2025, MeitY: DPDP Rules, 2025.

This page describes our practices. It does not claim that the site has been assessed or certified as compliant with any law.
