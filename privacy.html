Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

AI Tools Hub is a free directory of AI tools. We keep things simple: no accounts, no sign-ups and no forms.

What we collect

We do not ask for or store your name, email, phone number or any personal details. The site has no login and no database.

On your device

Your theme choice (light or dark) and tools you save or compare are kept only in your own browser for your visit. You can clear them any time by clearing your browser data.

Third-party services

Tool logos are loaded from Google's favicon service, and a small scrolling script loads from the jsDelivr CDN. These services may see your IP address and browser details when they send those files, under their own privacy policies. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs.

Links to other websites

Every tool listed links to its own website. Once you leave AI Tools Hub, that site's own privacy policy applies. We are not responsible for how other websites handle your data.

Children

The site is meant for business users and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
