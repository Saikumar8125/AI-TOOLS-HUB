04FAQ

Which is cheapest to start?

AiSensy has a Free Forever plan; paid plans start at ₹1,500/month. Message charges are extra.

Are WhatsApp message charges included?

No. All three state Meta message/template charges are extra.

Can customers pay by UPI inside WhatsApp?

AiSensy lists payments via UPI and card; Interakt lists native WhatsApp Pay; Wati offers WhatsApp Pay API on its Business plan.

Which works with Shopify?

All three: AiSensy and Interakt integrate directly; Wati needs a $4.99/month Shopify add-on.

Is there a free trial?

AiSensy has a free plan, Wati a 7-day trial, Interakt a free trial of unstated length.
