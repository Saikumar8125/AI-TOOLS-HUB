FAQ

Does Amazon India have its own free AI tools for sellers?

Yes: Seller Assistant (English and Hinglish), AI listing creation, Enhance My Listing, A+ content AI for Brand Registered sellers, and the Ads Image Generator. All are free.

Does Helium 10 work for amazon.in?

Partly. Its help centre lists keyword research, Listing Analyzer, Ads, Follow-Up and Profits for amazon.in, but not Listing Builder. Billing is in USD.

Does Jungle Scout support Amazon India?

Only "limited support". Seasonality data isn't available for India.

Which Amazon seller tools are priced in rupees?

Insydz (₹0 / ₹1,999 / ₹2,999 a month) and Zoho Books (from ₹749 a month, billed annually). Helium 10, SellerApp and Jungle Scout publish USD prices.

Do I need Brand Registry for the A+ content AI?

Yes.
