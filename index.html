      Questions, answered

      What is AI Tools Hub?

      A directory of 585 hand-picked AI tools across 26 categories, curated for Indian small businesses: shop owners, restaurants, CA firms and D2C brands. Free, freemium and paid tools, each linking to its official site.

      What do Free, Freemium and Paid mean?

      Free: completely free to use. Freemium: a free plan, with a paid plan for extra features. Paid: a paid tool, some with a free trial. Prices change often, so check the official site for current plans.

      What do the India statuses mean?

      Every tool has one of three India statuses. Verified for India: INR pricing, UPI payments, GST invoices, Indian-language support or Made in India was confirmed on the tool's official pages (checked 11 Oct 2026). India availability not verified: we have not confirmed it yet, so check the official site. Not available in India: used only when an official source confirms it.

      How does the Tool Finder choose tools?

      It matches your task against each tool's name, category, highlights and description using a fixed synonym list and fixed weights, then applies your pricing, budget and feature choices. The same input always gives the same result, and every pick shows why it matched. Budget maps to pricing models only, because the directory does not hold INR prices.

      How are tools chosen and checked?

      Every tool is hand-picked before it is listed. The last full link check was on 27 Oct 2026. New tools are added as they launch. Spot a wrong link or price? Report it on GitHub.

      Where do the links go?

      Every Visit button opens the tool's official website in a new tab.

      Is my data stored?

      Saved tools, compare picks, recently viewed tools and your theme are kept only in this browser (localStorage). Nothing is sent to a server. Suggestions and reports open a public GitHub issue that you submit yourself.
